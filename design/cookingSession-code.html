Crispy Cast Iron Chicken Thighs with Garlic Pan Sauce

A foolproof slow-render technique delivering deeply crackling skin, aromatic pan juices, and tender meat using one single skillet.

Glanceable instructions tuned for kitchen counters. Tap "Mark Step Done" as you cook.

STEP 01

Season chicken generously on all sides with salt and black pepper. Let sit at room temperature for 15 minutes to take off chill.

STEP 02

Place thighs skin-side down in a cold cast iron skillet. Turn heat to medium-low to slowly render fat until skin is deep golden brown and crisp (approx 14 minutes).

lightbulb Chef's secret: Starting cold releases fat without burning the skin. Do not touch or move them until naturally released!
STEP 03 thermostat 165°F (74°C)

Flip chicken thighs, add smashed garlic cloves and thyme sprigs. Cook for another 6–8 minutes until internal temperature registers 165°F (74°C).

STEP 04 dinner_dining Finish & Sauce

Transfer chicken to a warm plate. Deglaze skillet with white wine, scraping up browned bits, and simmer until sauce thickens. Spoon glossy sauce around chicken and garnish with parsley.
